🏛️ Unified Agricultural Land Valuation

Government Circle Rates vs Market Rates

Access official state stamp duty ready reckoner guidance values side-by-side with supplementary real estate asking prices. Transparent, verified, and normalized to standard rates per acre.

🏛️
Government Circle Rate (Authoritative) Official minimum registration value fixed by State Revenue & Registration Departments (IGR Maharashtra, Kaveri Karnataka, Telangana CARD, UP IGRS). Rates revised annually each fiscal year on April 1.
🏷️
Market Rate (Supplementary Listing) Aggregated asking prices from 99acres and MagicBricks agricultural listings. Subject to buyer-seller negotiation; labeled strictly as asking price, not final deed transaction value.
