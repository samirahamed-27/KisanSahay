Aadhaar-Bank Linking is Mandatory

Aadhaar linking is strictly required for all government loan schemes, interest subventions, and Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT). Loans cannot be applied for until your bank account is Aadhaar seeded.

1
Visit Bank Branch

Visit your bank branch with your original Aadhaar card and passbook for instant biometric authentication.

2
Link via ATM

Insert your debit card at any bank ATM → Go to Services → Select "Aadhaar Registration / Seeding".

3
SMS / NetBanking

Use your bank's official internet banking or SMS format to link your Aadhaar number instantly.

Check your live Aadhaar-Bank seeding status: 🏛️ Check on UIDAI Portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) ↗
